Process

A Structured, Documented, Fully Transparent Process From First Contact to Final Report

When you engage my services, you are not entering a vague, undefined relationship where you send money and hope for results. You are entering a structured professional partnership with clearly defined stages, documented communication, measurable deliverables, and complete accountability at every step.

01

Project Inquiry Submission

The process begins when you submit your project inquiry through the secure inquiry form on this website. Your inquiry should include your book title, genre, current marketing situation, and the primary goals you are hoping to achieve. Once received, your inquiry is reviewed in full and you will receive a professional acknowledgment email within 48 business hours.

02

Project Scope Assessment

Based on your inquiry, I conduct an initial assessment of your project, reviewing your book, your current Amazon presence, your author platform, your competitive landscape, and your stated goals. This assessment informs the scope of work and ensures that any proposal submitted to you is accurate, realistic, and specifically tailored to your book's actual needs.

03

Formal Proposal Delivery

A detailed written proposal is delivered to you via email. This proposal outlines the specific scope of work, the services to be delivered, the timeline for each phase of the project, and the terms of engagement. Every element of the proposal is clearly documented so that both parties have a complete and shared understanding.

04

Agreement and Project Activation

Upon your review and acceptance of the proposal, a formal project agreement is confirmed via email. Once confirmed, your project is officially activated and work begins according to the agreed timeline.

05

Research and Strategy Development

The foundation of every campaign is research. Before any marketing execution begins, I conduct the full research and analysis phase, covering market analysis, competitive positioning, reader persona development, keyword research, and platform audits. The completed strategy is documented and shared with you via email for your review and alignment.

06

Campaign Execution

With strategy confirmed, full campaign execution begins. Every action taken on your behalf is executed according to the strategic plan, with real-time monitoring and performance tracking in place from day one. You are updated regularly throughout this phase through structured, professional email reports.

07

Performance Reporting and Optimization

Throughout the duration of your project, you receive regular detailed performance reports via email. These reports document exactly what has been done, what results have been achieved, what the data indicates, and what optimizations are being implemented in response. Nothing is assumed. Nothing is vague. Everything is in writing.

08

Project Completion and Final Performance Summary

At the conclusion of your project, you receive a comprehensive Final Performance Summary, a complete documented record of every action taken, every result achieved, every metric tracked, and every insight gained throughout the campaign.
